Problem Statement

The agricultural retail sector heavily relies on traditional, manual workflows. Currently, there is no simple, hyper-local platform that seamlessly connects farmers with verified local agricultural retailers while combining product discovery, inventory visibility, and digital credit.

Our project specifically targets the severe operational frictions present in daily agri-retail, which include:

  • Price & Stock Uncertainty: Farmers travel to multiple physical shops without prior knowledge of product availability or local pricing.
  • Fragile Paper "Bahi-Khata" Systems: Manual credit records create an unreliable financial environment that is difficult to maintain and preserve against disputes.
  • Time & Fuel Loss: Unplanned trips to distant agri-retailers lead to a substantial loss of time and fuel, hurting farmer productivity.
  • Vendor Digital Limitations: Local agricultural retailers currently lack accessible digital tools for real-time inventory management.

Project Description

Jaymani Krishi Sewa is a comprehensive, digital agritech marketplace that bridges the gap between farmers and local agricultural vendors. The primary scope of this project is to integrate hyper-local product discovery, real-time inventory synchronization, and secure digital credit management (Bahi-Khata) into a single, unified web application.

Project Modules: Design & Algorithm

The system is logically partitioned into five interdependent modules:

  • Authentication & Role-Based Access Module: A unified gateway that manages secure logins for all users while strictly isolating data based on user types.
  • Hyper-Local Discovery & Search Module (Farmer): The core search engine that allows farmers to find nearby agricultural supplies dynamically.
  • Vendor Inventory & Catalog Module (Supply): A robust dashboard giving retailers control over their digital storefront and stock levels.
  • Digital Order & Cart Processing Module: Handles the transactional phase where demand meets supply seamlessly without stock conflicts.
  • Digital "Bahi-Khata" (Credit Ledger) Module: Digitizes the traditional, paper-based trust system between farmers and local retailers into a secure financial ledger.

Implementation Methodology

The system is being developed using an Iterative and Phased approach. This methodology ensures continuous refinement across our 8 core milestones: Requirement Analysis, UI/UX Design, Backend/Database setup, Frontend Development, System Integration, Testing, and Deployment.

Result & Conclusion

Jaymani Krishi Sewa successfully conceptualizes a highly practical, modern solution to the operational frictions inherent in traditional agricultural retail. The main achievement of this project lies in creating a unified ecosystem that simultaneously connects local farmer demand with verified local vendor supply while eliminating critical pain points like blind shop visits and time loss.

The most significant innovation of this project is the modernization of the traditional rural credit system by digitizing the "Bahi-Khata" (credit ledger). In conclusion, Jaymani Krishi Sewa stands out by empowering existing micro-local economies and optimizing supply chain efficiency.
